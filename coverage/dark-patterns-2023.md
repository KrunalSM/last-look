# Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns 2023
- source-id: dark-patterns-2023
- walk: full
- url: https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/The%20Guidelines%20for%20Prevention%20and%20Regulation%20of%20Dark%20Patterns%2C%202023_1732707717.pdf
- read-on: 2026-11-03

Notification F. No. CCPA-1/1/2023-CCPA-(Reg) of 30 November 2023 by the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) under s.18 of the Consumer Protection Act 2019, Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part III s.4, No. 783 (CG-DL-E-30112023-250339). It is in force on publication (cl.1(2)), so from 30 November 2023. The copy read is the Gazette itself, linked from the CCPA's Guidelines and Advisories page (https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/guidelines.php, "Last Updated: 29-09-2026"), which lists no amendment. No notified amendment and no newly specified dark pattern (cl.2(1)(i)) was found; a PIB release of 3 June 2026 still speaks of 13 dark patterns.

Guidance, not an amendment: the CCPA advisory of 5 June 2025 asks e-commerce platforms to self-audit for dark patterns within 3 months and encourages self-declarations (https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/Advisory-7.pdf). It adds no check, because it asks for an internal audit rather than a feature. From 1 January 2027 the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules 2026 make following these Guidelines, a yearly self-audit and a displayed certificate a duty of e-commerce entities (r.4(15)); that is mapped in the E-Commerce Rules walk.

Enforcement example (official press release, not legal text): on 3 June 2026 the CCPA fined one platform for a pre-selected donation with emotional messages and for "free" courses gated behind phone and email (basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action), and another for a "Renew Now" versus "Accept Risk" renewal screen (confirm shaming, interface interference, trick question, forced action): https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2268302

Strength. Annexure 1 says its practices and illustrations "provide only guidance" and are not a binding interpretation; cl.4 and cl.5 make engaging in an Annexure 1 practice a dark pattern. Rules use Court or regulator.

Drip pricing (Annexure 1(8)) and disguised advertisement (1(9)) are mapped to checks from the E-Commerce walk (price-breakdown-missing, sponsored-listing-unlabelled), so no duplicate checks were made. From 1 January 2027 the E-Commerce Rules r.4(15) also make following these Guidelines a legal duty for e-commerce entities; those Law records (ecommerce-r4-15-*) sit with the E-Commerce rules.
