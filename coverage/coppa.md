Read on eCFR (Title 16 up to date as of 30 October 2026), the amended Rule in the Federal Register (90 FR 16918, 22 April 2025: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule) and the FTC FAQ (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions, staff views, not binding; read in a browser because ftc.gov refuses scripted requests).

Dates. The Rule took effect on 21 April 2000 (90 FR 16918). The amended Rule is effective 23 June 2025, and regulated entities have until 22 April 2026 to comply, except for parts of the safe harbour section (312.11(d)(1), (d)(4) and (g)). The FAQ header still says the Rule "was amended on April 22, 2025", the publication date. FAQ A.12 keeps "The Rule does not require operators to ask the age of visitors".

Penalties. FAQ B.2 gives up to $53,088 per violation. That is the FTC Act s.5(m)(1)(A) amount in 16 CFR 1.98(d) (90 FR 5581, 17 January 2025); the FTC made no 2026 adjustment (91 FR 58446, 15 September 2026) because OMB cancelled it.
