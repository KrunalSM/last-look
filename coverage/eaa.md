# European Accessibility Act, Directive (EU) 2019/882
- source-id: eaa
- walk: targeted
- url: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng
- read-on: 2026-11-03

Targeted walk: only the parts the accessibility checks rely on. Each of the six accessibility checks carries one EAA rule (`eaa-annex1-<check-id>`), scoped to the services in Art. 2(2) and excluding microenterprises (Art. 4(5)). Text read from the Official Journal copy served by the EU Publications Office (http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32019L0882), because EUR-Lex refuses scripted requests.

Facts read for the mapping: the Directive covers listed services provided to consumers after 28 June 2025 (Art. 2(2)), including e-commerce services and consumer banking; Member States apply their national measures from 28 June 2025 (Art. 31(2)); microenterprises providing services (fewer than 10 staff and turnover or balance sheet of EUR 2 million or less, Art. 3) are exempt from the service requirements (Art. 4(5)); services under contracts made before 28 June 2025 may continue unchanged until they end, for at most five years, and products already used to provide services may be used until 28 June 2030 (Art. 32).
