# RBI Digital Payments - E-mandate Framework, 2026
- source-id: rbi-recurring-payments
- walk: targeted
- url: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13374
- read-on: 2026-11-03

Targeted walk of the whole Master Direction (11 numbered paragraphs): RBI/DPSS/2026-27/396, CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S56/02.14.003/2026-27, 21 April 2026, issued under s.10(2) read with s.18 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007 and "effective immediately" (para 1(b)). It consolidates and repeals eight e-mandate circulars from 21 August 2019 to 22 August 2024 (para 11), including the limits of 16 June 2022 (Rs 15,000) and 12 December 2023 (Rs 1,00,000 for some categories).

The duties fall on payment system providers and participants (issuers, acquirers, payment aggregators). Para 10(c) makes acquirers ensure that their merchants comply, so a merchant meets it by using the gateway's subscription or mandate flow. RBI card rules other than e-mandates (for example tokenisation) are not part of this walk.
