Targeted walk: only the sections the checks rely on. Read on law.cornell.edu (17 U.S.C. 512, 504, 412) and eCFR (37 CFR 201.3 and 201.38, current as of 30 October 2026). Start date: title II of Pub. L. 105-304 took effect on enactment, 28 October 1998 (effective-date note under s.512).

Agent. The s.512(c) safe harbour applies only if the service has designated an agent, on its website in a public place and with the Copyright Office (s.512(c)(2)). The designation expires three years after registration unless renewed (37 CFR 201.38(c)(4)); the fee for a designation, amendment or renewal is $6 (37 CFR 201.3(c), item 25).

Damages and s.412. Statutory damages run from $750 to $30,000 per work, up to $150,000 if the owner proves willful infringement (s.504(c)(1)-(2)). s.412 bars statutory damages and attorney's fees for infringement of an unpublished work begun before registration, or of a published work begun before registration unless the work is registered within three months of first publication. So the risk of a missing agent is losing the safe harbour; the largest damages need both willfulness and timely registration by the owner.
