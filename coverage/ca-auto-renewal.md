Targeted walk of the whole article (7 sections, with s.17602 split by subdivision). Read on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov in a browser (scripted requests get 403). This replaces the california.public.law copy the seed rule used.

Dates. The article became operative on 1 December 2010 (s.17606). AB 2863 (Stats. 2024, ch. 515) amended s.17601 and s.17602, effective 1 January 2025, for contracts entered into, amended or extended on or after 1 July 2025 (s.17601(b), s.17602(j)).

Software and the s.17603 gift clause. The article covers goods and services: s.17600 names "ongoing deliveries of service", and the s.17601 definitions speak of goods or services. s.17603 is narrower: when a business "sends any goods, wares, merchandise, or products" without the consent s.17602 requires, they are an unconditional gift. It does not name services, software or digital content, and no definition extends it to them. Read from the text alone, the gift clause is not shown to cover a software or digital subscription, so a report must not claim a refund right under s.17603; the s.17602 duties still apply to such subscriptions. No court reading was checked. s.17604: a violation is not a crime, civil remedies apply, and good-faith compliance shields from them.
