Amendments, from the note to G.S.R. 120(E) and the consolidated footnotes. G.S.R. 139(E) of 25 Feb 2021: the principal rules, in force on publication (2021-02-25). G.S.R. 794(E) of 28 Oct 2022, in force the same day: language of the user's choice, r.3(1)(m) and (n), new grievance clocks, Grievance Appellate Committees (r.3A). G.S.R. 275(E) of 6 Apr 2023: online gaming, the fact check unit words in r.3(1)(b)(v), the meaning of "prominently publish"; its start day is not stated in the texts read. G.S.R. 775(E) of 22 Oct 2025, in force 15 Nov 2025: who may send a takedown notice under r.3(1)(d), and what it must say. G.S.R. 120(E) of 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026: synthetically generated information (AI-made audio, images and video), reminders every three months, takedown in 3 hours (was 36), complaints resolved in 7 days (was 15), removal requests in 36 hours (was 72), intimate images in 2 hours (was 24). The MeitY FAQ of 10 Feb 2026 lists the same four amendments; no later notified amendment was found.

Out of scope, because drafts are not law: the draft Second Amendment Rules 2026 published for comment on 30 Mar 2026 (compliance with Ministry advisories as due diligence, retention, news posted by non-publishers) and the further draft change to r.3(3)(a)(ii) noticed on 21 Apr 2026. No Gazette notification of either was found; a press report of 24 Aug 2026 (secondary) still describes them as under consideration.

Courts. r.3(1)(b)(v), fact check unit words: the Bombay High Court declared them unconstitutional on 20 Sep 2024, and on 10 Mar 2026 the Supreme Court issued notice on the Union's appeal and refused to stay that judgment (secondary: https://www.scobserver.in/reports/sc-issues-notice-in-unions-appeal-on-it-rules-2023/). The consolidated text still prints the words. r.9(1) and r.9(3): the Bombay High Court stayed them in an interim order reported on 14 Aug 2021 (secondary: https://www.barandbench.com/news/litigation/it-rules-2021-bombay-high-court-interim-protection-rule-9); their later status was not confirmed.

Online gaming. Under r.4B the rule 3 and rule 4 duties for online games don't apply until three months after at least three online gaming self-regulatory bodies are designated under r.4A, unless the Government notifies an earlier date; no designation was found in the texts read, so those duties are treated as not yet applying and are never confirmed. Real-money games are also governed by the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025, which law firm reports say is in force from 1 May 2026 and which may ban online money games; that Act is not walked here.
