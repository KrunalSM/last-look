# CAN-SPAM Act, 15 U.S.C. 7701-7713, and CAN-SPAM Rule, 16 CFR Part 316

- walk: targeted
- url: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7704

Targeted walk: only the sections the checks rely on. Read on law.cornell.edu (15 U.S.C. 7702, 7704, 7706), eCFR (16 CFR Part 316 and 1.98), the Federal Register and the FTC compliance guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business, read in a browser because ftc.gov refuses scripted requests). This replaces the guide as the rule's source.

Start date: the sections are effective 1 January 2004 (Pub. L. 108-187 s.16, as noted under 7702 and 7704).

Penalties: the guide says each email in violation can cost up to $53,088. That is the FTC Act s.5(m)(1)(A) amount in 16 CFR 1.98(d) (90 FR 5581, 17 January 2025). For 2026 the FTC made no adjustment: OMB cancelled the 2026 adjustments because the October 2025 CPI-U was not produced during the shutdown, and the FTC keeps the 2025 levels (FTC notice, 91 FR 58446, 15 September 2026: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/15/2026-18853/civil-penalty-inflation-adjustments).
