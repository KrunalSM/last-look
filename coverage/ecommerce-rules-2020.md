Out of scope: the draft amendments published for comment in June 2021 were never notified, so they are not law. Searches found no other notified amendment.

Who is a consumer: Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.2(7) (https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2019/210422.pdf). A person who buys goods for resale or any commercial purpose, or avails services for any commercial purpose, is not a consumer; use to earn a livelihood by self-employment is not commercial; online purchases count. The Rules are enforced through the Act (r.8), so sales only to businesses are treated as likely out of scope. r.2(1) itself does not limit the Rules to consumers, so the checks say "likely".
